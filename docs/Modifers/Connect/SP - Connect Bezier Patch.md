Modifier to connect one side of the current patch to one side of a target patch.

<img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/7c4c691e-7a23-41f9-a48e-3825f3ba8716" height="400">

### Target
Can be another bezier patch, a curve or a flat patch. NURBS support is in progress.

### Continuity level
Currently high continuity is only available on connection with natural patch boundaries.

### Tension
Tension has no effect under G0 continuity.
