## Trim Contour
### Scaling Method
Method to interpret the trim contour coordinates.
* Fit Contour : Adjusts depending on drawn contour. 
* UV : [0,1]x[0,1] Range

## Exact Normals
Normals generated by default are bent at the border. You can fix this issue by enabling "Exact Normal" (disabled by default because of poor performances).

<img src="https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f62e4e63-f62e-41bd-bd23-af5612083b34" height="350">

It generates an attribute "SP_Normals" you can reuse directly in your materials or with the "SP - Patch Normals" asset node group used as normal of your material
