`MODIFIER` [`MESHING`](https://github.com/RomainGuimbal/SurfacePsycho/wiki/3.-Modifers-and-Tools#meshing-modifiers)

To mesh surfaces of extrusion. Extrusion surfaces are a separate type in the STEP norm. Part of the benefit of using them compared to just a ruled surface (degree 1 in the extrusion direction), is  that you can extrude any type of segment (circles, ellipses...).
