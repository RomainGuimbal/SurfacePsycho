This modifier allow to extrude curves or flat patches in a specified direction. The extrusion result has several surfaces, meaning the modifier must be on a compound object.
- The extruded curve or flat patch can either be from a separate object target, or from the mesh of the compound itself (`Extrude Self` option), in which case no target is required.
- The direction and length of the extrusion are either controlled by the `Direction` and `Offset` parameters or by an edge in the mesh if `Use Vertices as Offset` is enabled.
