This tool allows for a visual check of meshed surface curvatures in every direction (curvature is not only Gaussian or mean curvature). This tool should allow you to close your eyes at night if you are a surface psychopath as I am.

Be aware it depends on mesh resolution of your patches. The sample radius is the zone on witch the curvature radius is determined, so on a sparse mesh prefer a large radius for better accuracy.

[![Curvature Probe usage on Suzanne](https://github.com/RomainGuimbal/SurfacePsycho/assets/39882829/12d448fd-ddd6-4f6b-9917-78cc9e2a9e6f)](https://github.com/RomainGuimbal/SurfacePsycho/assets/39882829/8519c398-4fdb-44e0-be03-995f2a1cfaa7)

To chose a surface to probe use the eyedropper on 1 or 2 objects or chose a collection (or do both).
